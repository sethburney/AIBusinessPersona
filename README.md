# AI Business Persona

A personal AI persona with a face, a story, and a clear mission: teaching business workers how to use AI in their day-to-day jobs through social media posts.

**Status:** Persona chosen: **Remi** (`@remitobor`). Profile, backstory, and likeness kit drafted (v1). Open items are marked **[Confirm]** or _Pending_.

## Project structure

```
persona/                  Who the persona is
  00-concepts.md          Draft persona directions to choose from
  01-profile.md           Name, role, and a quick-reference card
  02-backstory.md         Origin story and why they teach
  03-focus-and-goals.md   Audience, topics, and what success means
  04-voice-and-style.md   How they write and sound
  05-visual-identity.md   Face, look, likeness profile, and styling
  06-generation-prompts.md  Image and video prompts and review checklist
  07-likeness-consent.md  Consent scope checklist for the likeness
content/                  What the persona posts
  content-pillars.md      Recurring themes the posts rotate through
  content-calendar.md     Posting schedule and planned posts
  post-template.md        Standard format for drafting a post
  drafts/                 Posts in progress
  published/              Archive of posts that went live
assets/face/              Approved persona images (reference photos stay local and are git-ignored)
research/                 Audience research, competitor notes, sources
GUARDRAILS.md             Transparency, accuracy, and platform rules
DECISIONS.md              Log of key decisions and the reasons for them
```

## Suggested order of work

1. **Guardrails first.** Decide how the persona will say it is AI (see `GUARDRAILS.md`). This affects the story, the face, and the bio.
2. **Focus and goals.** Who the audience is and what success looks like.
3. **Profile and backstory.** The character, built to serve the focus.
4. **Voice and visual identity.** How the persona sounds and looks.
5. **Content pillars and calendar.** What gets posted, where, and how often.
