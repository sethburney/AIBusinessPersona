# Content Calendar

- **Platforms:** Instagram and TikTok first; repurpose to Facebook, YouTube Shorts, and X. YouTube long-form every other week.
- **Base cadence:** 3 core posts per week (Mon, Wed, Fri) plus text versions for X and Facebook the same day.
- **Rhythm:** One pillar per week, 7-week cycle. Cycle 1 uses the strongest lessons; cycle 2 uses the rest (see below).

- **Best posting times:** _TBD_ (use the platforms' own analytics after the first 2–4 weeks)

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Series | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Level-Up Monday | Talking-head or screen-share reel (30–60 s) | One skill from the week's pillar |
| **Wed** | The Playbook | Carousel (6–10 slides); thread on X | The step-by-step: how my human's team did it |
| **Fri** | Flip It / Remi's Oops / The Handoff / Steal This Prompt (rotate) | Reel, carousel, or prompt card | Before/after, a real lesson, a hand-off, or a starter prompt |
| **Every other Thu** | Deep Dive | YouTube, 8–12 min | Full walkthrough of one pillar |

> **Production note:** Talking-head reels need the lip-sync setup and her OK for lip-synced video and an AI voice (`persona/07-likeness-consent.md`). Until then, Monday reels can be screen-share or voiceover style, or swap in a carousel.
