## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Series | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Level-Up Monday | Talking-head or screen-share reel (30–60 s) | One skill from the week's pillar |
| **Wed** | The Playbook | Carousel (6–10 slides); thread on X | The step-by-step: how my human's team did it |
| **Fri** | Flip It / Remi's Oops / The Handoff (rotate) | Reel or carousel | Before/after, a real lesson, or how it was handed to the team |
| **Every other Thu** | Deep Dive | YouTube, 8–12 min | Full walkthrough of one pillar |

> **Production note:** Talking-head reels need the lip-sync setup and her OK for lip-synced video and an AI voice (`persona/07-likeness-consent.md`). Until then, Monday reels can be screen-share or voiceover style, or swap in a carousel.
