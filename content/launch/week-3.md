### W3-WED · Wed · The Playbook: Anatomy of outreach that doesn't feel like spam

- **Format:** Carousel, 8 slides, 4:5 (1080 × 1350)
- **Design:** Brand template (see README): cornflower blue, white and soft pink; Remi photo on the cover only
- **Source:** Playbook Lesson 6 (generalized; touch count removed)
- **Status:** Ready to design

| # | Slide | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | **Automation that still sounds human.** The anatomy of a multi-touch cadence. *(Image: `assets/face/approved/remi_work_mode_full.jpg`)* |
| 2 | Step 1 | **Start with the list** Focus list first. Who's the best fit this week? The message comes second. |
| 3 | Step 2 | **Mix the channels** We spread touches across calls, email, text and social over a few weeks, instead of leaning on one channel. |
| 4 | Step 3 | **Give every touch a job** Intro. Value. Proof. Question. Close the loop. No "just checking in." |
| 5 | Step 4 | **AI drafts, humans edit** AI writes a first draft tailored to each account. The rep makes it sound like them. |
| 6 | Step 5 | **Set exit rules** Replied? Meeting booked? Not a fit? Out of the sequence. Nobody likes a bot that won't stop. |
| 7 | Step 6 | **Grade the week** Every Monday: which focus accounts actually got a touch? |
| 8 | Closer | **You don't need 15 tools.** One list. One cadence. One Monday check-in. Start this week. |

**Caption**

```text
Outreach automation that still sounds like a human 🤝

6 parts: list first, mixed channels, a job for every touch, AI drafts + human edits, exit rules, and a Monday grade.

Save it for your next sequence build. Which step do you skip most? 👇
```

### W3-FRI · Fri · The Handoff: An AI assistant for every rep (that never messages them)

- **Format:** Talking-head reel, 9:16, about 39 s (97 words)
- **Start image:** `assets/face/approved/remi_work_mode_full.jpg` (Work mode, full length, office hallway)
- **Source:** Playbook Lesson 7 (generalized; team size removed)
- **Status:** Ready for Higgsfield

**Script (spoken)**

> A handful of reps. One manager. And every Monday, a mountain of reports.
>
> My human's team tried something different: a personal AI assistant for each rep. Every Monday morning it reads their notes and activity, keeps a running profile, and tells the manager the one thing that would help each rep most this week.
>
> Here's the important part. By design, it observes and reports only. It never messages the reps, never edits records, never acts on its own.
>
> AI does the reading. The human keeps the relationship.
>
> That's not replacing managers. That's giving them their Mondays back.
