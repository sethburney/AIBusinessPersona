# Read-Aloud Sign-Off

Check 4 and 5 of the Human Voice Check (`STYLE-GUIDE.md`). Every talking video gets read out loud by the owner and by the owner's wife before it goes to Higgsfield.

**How:** read each script once at normal speed, without practicing. Mark anywhere you stumbled, ran out of breath, or thought "nobody talks like that." Write a better way to say it in your own words; your wording beats both AIs. Then check your box.

Carousels aren't read aloud, but a quick read of the slide copy is welcome.

> Got a pile of reports to read before you can help your team?
>
> I'm Remi, your AI work bestie. Here's what my human's team tried.
>
> Each rep got an AI assistant. Every Monday, it read their notes and activity.
>
> Then it suggested where the manager could help most.

> Here's the important part: it could read and report. That's it.

>
> The manager checked the summary and talked with the rep.
>
> And managers got their Mondays back.
>
> Try it: get permission, remove names, and ask AI to summarize one week of notes.
