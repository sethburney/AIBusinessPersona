# Content Pillars

Seven pillars from the owner's real implementations and the owner's 24-lesson playbook (`research/AI-Playbook-for-Business-Professionals.md`), tied together by one framework: **Build It, Prove It, Hand It Off** (see `messaging-framework.md`). Lesson-by-lesson post ideas: `research/playbook-content-map.md`.

| # | Pillar | What it covers | Playbook lessons | Example post | Digital product tie-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clean data, connected systems** | CRM sync, contact cleansing, data standards, pipeline design | 8, 10, 11 | "What if your report found the errors for you?" | CRM cleanup checklist |
| 2 | **Outreach that scales** | Focus lists, sequences, automated outreach | 6, 7 | "Your reps don't need a database. They need a list." | Sequence templates, outreach prompt pack |
| 3 | **Leads that convert** | MQL definition, nurture email, ad spend to CRM outcomes | 18 | "Your ad platform grades its own homework." | MQL worksheet, nurture templates |
| 4 | **Sell smarter** | Tailored decks, packaged analysis, playbook audits, coaching, renewals | 1–5 | "Why show every prospect the same deck?" | Demo outline, call-audit prompts, renewal templates |
| 5 | **Local content that's actually local** | Geo-targeted content, content at scale, quality checks | 20 | "The swap test" | Local page template, editor prompt |
| 6 | **Getting found** | SEO and AEO: interlinking, backlinks, image/video, indexing, AI visibility | 21 | "Customers are asking ChatGPT. Are you in the answer?" | SEO/AEO checklist, AI visibility tracker |
| 7 | **Measure, test, trust your numbers** | A/B tests with control groups, data-truth audits, root cause, forecasts, commissions | 12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 19 | "In our experience, about 1 in 3 key metrics had a definition problem." | Dashboard template, A/B test planner |
| ↻ | **The Handoff** (runs through all seven) | Efficient leadership: track what matters, document it, hand it off, decide fast | 9, 14, 22, 23, 24 | "When AI speeds up the work, you become the bottleneck." | SOP template, 30-Day AI Level-Up |

**Recurring series that cut across pillars:** Steal This Prompt (24 starter prompts), Remi's Oops (7 "rules we learned the hard way"), Ask → Build → Delegate, 30-Day AI Level-Up.

**Mix target:** roughly equal time across pillars 1–7 over each 7-week cycle, with The Handoff closing most topic weeks.
