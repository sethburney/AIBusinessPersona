# Content Pillars

Six topics from the owner's real implementations, all tied together by one framework: **Build It, Prove It, Hand It Off** (see `messaging-framework.md`).

| # | Pillar | What it covers | Example post | Digital product tie-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clean data, connected systems** | CRM sync, contact cleansing, data hygiene | "5 signs your CRM data is costing you deals" | CRM cleanup checklist |
| 2 | **Outreach that scales** | Sequence building, automated messaging and outreach | "Flip It: manual follow-up to a 5-step sequence" | Sequence templates, outreach prompt pack |
| 3 | **Leads that convert** | MQL definition, lead scoring, nurture email strategy | "What actually makes a lead an MQL?" | MQL worksheet, nurture templates |
| 4 | **Local content that's actually local** | Geo-targeted, customized content and relevance checks | "City-swap pages vs. genuinely local pages" | Local page template |
| 5 | **Getting found** | SEO and AEO: interlinking, backlinking, image/video optimization, indexing | "Is Google even seeing your page?" | SEO/AEO pre-publish checklist |
| 6 | **Measure, test, scale** | Tracking SEO/AEO, scaling page output, A/B testing | "The 5 numbers we watch weekly" | Dashboard template, A/B test planner |
| ↻ | **The Handoff** (runs through all six) | Efficient leadership: track what matters, document it, hand it to the team | "What would break if you were out for two weeks?" | SOP template |

**Mix target:** roughly equal time across pillars 1–6 over each 6-week cycle, with The Handoff closing every topic week.
