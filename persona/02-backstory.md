# Backstory

> The story gives followers a reason to care and a lens for the content. It must follow the honesty rules in GUARDRAILS.md: no fake real-world credentials, employers, or results.

## Questions to answer

1. **Origin:** Where does the persona "come from"? Why do they exist?
2. **The problem they saw:** What frustration with AI at work do they want to fix? (e.g., overwhelm, fear of replacement, wasted time, hype vs. reality)
3. **Why they teach:** What drives them to help business workers specifically?
4. **Relatable struggle:** What do they find hard or get wrong? (A flawless character is less relatable.)
5. **Worldview on AI:** Enthusiast, pragmatist, or skeptic-turned-user? What do they believe about AI and jobs?
6. **Connection to the owner:** Is the owner's own story part of this, or is the persona separate?
