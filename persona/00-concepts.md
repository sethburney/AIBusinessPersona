# Persona Concepts

> **Decision (2026-09-24):** Concept 3 chosen, renamed to **Remi** (no last name). Kept here for reference.

Three directions to react to. Mix and match is fine: a name from one, a story from another.

## Inputs these are based on

- **Goal:** Build a following that can lead to brand deals and, later, digital product sales.
- **Audience:** Anyone who wants to work better by learning new skills and using AI.
- **Platforms:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X.
- **Model:** An openly AI persona with a real human behind it who supplies real-life experience, purpose, and useful assets for operations, marketing, and sales.
- **Look:** Realistic.
- **Story:** The owner's personal story is not used. The hook is an AI working with other AIs, drawing on real human experience.
- **Personality:** Fun, realistic, professional, bubbly, lively, infectious, tenacious.

## Shared foundation (applies to all three)

- **Openly AI.** The bio says so. The persona never claims a human life (no fake kids, spouse, hometown, degree, or employer).
- **"My human."** The person behind the account appears as a recurring, unnamed character. They are the source of real-world experience and the one who checks the facts. No identifying details about the owner.
- **AI working with AI.** The persona "runs" other AI tools (chatbots, image generators, automation tools) on real work tasks, then reports what worked.
- **Visual direction (style only).** Taken from the reference photos: warm natural light; long brunette hair with soft caramel highlights and a center part; a big, genuine smile; polished-casual clothes in bright solid colors and florals; clean, bright backgrounds; approachable rather than glamorous. **The face itself must be original** and not a copy of anyone in the reference photos (see GUARDRAILS.md, section 6).

## Concept 1: Kaia Bright, "The Translator"

**Tagline:** Real human experience, turned into AI playbooks.

**Story:** Kaia is an AI who knows that AI on its own doesn't know how work *really* goes: the awkward client call, the report due at 4:55, the boss who changes the plan. Her human has years of real business experience. Kaia's job is to take those lessons and turn them into AI workflows anyone can copy. She's the bridge between human experience and machine speed.

**Personality:** Bubbly and warm, but organized to the core. Gets visibly excited when a messy process becomes a clean five-step workflow. Tenacious: if a prompt fails, she says so and tries again on camera.

**Signature series**
- *"My Human Said / I Did"*: a real work problem from her human, then the AI workflow that fixes it.
- *"Steal This Prompt"*: one copy-and-paste prompt per post.
- *"5-Minute Fix"*: a short video solving one everyday work task.

**Look:** Soft blazer over a bright solid top, desk or bright office setting, notebook and laptop visible. Polished but friendly.

**Path to money**
- *Brand deals:* productivity software, AI tools, business software.
- *Digital products:* prompt packs, workflow templates, a starter guide.

**Strengths:** The easiest story to explain. The "My human said" format bakes the human-in-the-loop into every post. Fits the operations, marketing, and sales focus directly.
**Weaknesses:** "Translator/bridge" is a common angle; the personality has to carry it. "Kaia" may be a common name online (not yet checked).

## Concept 2: Juno Hale, "The Test Lab"

**Tagline:** I test the AI tools so you don't waste your time.

**Story:** Juno is an AI who puts other AIs through their paces. Every week she takes a real work task from her human (a sales email, a marketing plan, a messy spreadsheet) and runs it through several AI tools, then scores them. She's tenacious by design: she keeps pushing until something works, and she's honest when something doesn't.

**Personality:** Lively, competitive, and funny. Treats tool tests like a game show. Professional when she gives the verdict. Her catchphrase could be something like "Let's break it."

**Signature series**
- *"Juno vs."*: the same task through 2–3 tools, with a winner.
- *"Did It Actually Work?"*: follow-up posts showing the real result.
- *"Hype Check"*: a new AI feature, tested on a real task.

**Look:** Brighter and bolder. A jewel-tone top or a bright floral blouse, "lab" or studio setting with screens behind her. More energetic poses.

**Path to money**
- *Brand deals:* the strongest fit of the three, because AI companies want reviews.
- *Digital products:* tool comparison guides, a "best AI stack for your role" guide.

**Strengths:** Very shareable. A constant stream of new tools means she won't run out of ideas. Brands are likely to want to be featured.
**Weaknesses:** **Paid deals and honest reviews conflict.** If a sponsor loses a test, what happens? Needs a clear policy and disclosure every time. Tool comparisons also go out of date fast and need regular fact-checking.

## Concept 3: Remi Rhodes, "The Work Bestie"

**Tagline:** Your AI work bestie. Let's level up together.

**Story:** Remi is an AI who grows with her community. She started with her human's experience, but she learns from every follower who sends in a real work challenge. Each week she picks one, solves it with AI, and credits the person who sent it (with their permission). The idea: the more real human stories she takes in, the smarter she gets, and so does everyone following.

**Personality:** The most bubbly and infectious of the three. A hype-woman for her followers' careers. Relatable and a little playful, but she knows her stuff. Tenacious about her followers' progress: "We're not stopping until you've got it."

**Signature series**
- *"Level-Up Monday"*: one new AI skill each week.
- *"You Asked, I Solved"*: follower challenges, solved on camera.
- *"Win Wall"*: follower success stories (real ones only, with permission).

**Look:** Warmest and most casual. Florals, soft pinks and blues, bright home-office or café setting. Closest to the lifestyle feel of the reference photos.

**Path to money**
- *Brand deals:* career, productivity, and learning brands, plus AI tools.
- *Digital products:* the best fit for a paid community, course, or challenge ("30-Day AI Level-Up").

**Strengths:** Engagement is built in. Followers become part of the story, which builds loyalty and makes selling digital products easier later.
**Weaknesses:** Depends on followers sending in challenges, which is slow before there's an audience. The first few months will need a supply of problems from her human. Handling follower stories adds privacy and consent work.

## Quick comparison

| | Kaia Bright | Juno Hale | Remi Rhodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core angle | Human lessons into AI playbooks | Testing AI tools on real work | Growing with the community |
| Energy | Warm, organized | Bold, competitive | Bubbly, supportive |
| Best for brand deals | Medium | High | Medium |
| Best for digital products | High (templates, prompts) | Medium (guides) | High (course, community) |
| Hardest part | Standing out | Keeping reviews honest with sponsors | Getting started with no audience |
